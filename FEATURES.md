# Kirana Khata: features (v1.1)

A free, offline app for shops: khata, bills, stock, cash and GST. Everything stays on the phone.

| Area | Features |
|------|----------|
| Khata | Customers and suppliers. Credit/Payment entries with automatic balances and a running balance. Edit, back-date, delete with undo. Opening balance and a photo of the page from the paper bahi. Due dates with Collections (overdue / today / upcoming). WhatsApp reminder, call, and a PDF statement. |
| Bills | Items with qty and unit, suggestions from earlier bills, paid / credit / part paid (the unpaid part goes to the khata), round off, a PDF with a status stamp, share or download. |
| GST & HSN (v1.1) | The bundled CBIC rate list (as on 22.09.2025, 1,367 rows). Each item links to a schedule and HSN row with its product nature and rate (overridable, price incl./excl. GST). Many products can share one HSN. Typing an HSN code in a search lists every product under it. Bills follow the shop's registration: **Regular** = Tax Invoice with GSTIN, HSN and CGST/SGST; **Composition** = Bill of Supply with GSTIN and no tax lines; **Not registered** = plain receipt. |
| Stock | Tracked items, bills reduce stock, deleting a bill restores it. Stock in/out, history, low-stock alerts, stock value. |
| Money | Expenses by category. Galla (daily cash in/out, filled in automatically). Reports for today / week / month / last month. |
| Safety | Android Google Drive auto backup, a backup file with restore, app lock (fingerprint / phone PIN). |
| Languages | Hinglish (default), English, Hindi. |

Not included: compensation cess, inter-state IGST, e-invoicing, thermal printing.
